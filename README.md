# Customer-Churn-Analysis-Dashboard

# Bank Customer Data | Power BI + Excel + DAX

# Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior in a banking dataset to understand why customers leave and which factors influence retention.
The goal was not just to build visuals but to derive business insights that can help organizations improve customer retention strategies.


# Business Objectives

* Identify customer segments most likely to churn
* Analyze impact of age, credit score, account balance, and activity status
* Evaluate how credit card ownership affects retention
* Convert raw customer data into meaningful business KPIs

# Dataset Information

* Total Customers: 10,000
* Churn Rate: ~20%
* Domain: Banking / Customer Retention Analysis

# Key Insights
# Customer Behavior

* Certain age groups showed higher churn probability
* Active customers had significantly lower churn rates

# Financial Factors

* Credit score and account balance influenced customer retention
* Customers without credit cards showed slightly higher churn trends

#  KPI Insights

* Overall churn rate ~20%
* Retention rate ~80%
* Clear segmentation helped highlight risk groups


# What I Explored

* Which age groups are more likely to churn
* Impact of credit score and account balance on retention
* Difference between active vs inactive customers
* Effect of credit card ownership on churn behavior

# Key Learning
This project improved my understanding of:-

* Customer behavior analysis
* KPI creation using DAX
* Data storytelling through dashboards
